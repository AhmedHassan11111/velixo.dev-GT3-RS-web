# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Potential clients — business owners, entrepreneurs, and creative directors who want a premium, visually striking website for their product or service. They encounter this site while evaluating whether velixo.io can deliver the level of craft they need. Their context: browsing on desktop, often via a shared link or portfolio reference, with a short attention span and high visual expectations. The job to be done: within 30 seconds, feel confident that velixo.io can produce work at or above the standard they see on this page.

## Product Purpose

A Porsche 911 GT3 RS showcase website that serves a dual purpose: (1) present the car with the reverence and production value it deserves, and (2) function as a living portfolio piece for velixo.io, proving that the developer can build immersive, animation-rich, premium web experiences. Success looks like a visitor scrolling the entire page, then clicking "Send Message" to inquire about their own project.

## Brand Personality

Premium, bold, precise.

- **Premium**: Every pixel earns its place. Materials, spacing, and motion feel considered — never accidental. The site should feel like it belongs next to the car, not behind it.
- **Bold**: Confident typography, full-bleed imagery, unapologetic use of space. No timidity in layout or color. The site takes up room.
- **Precise**: Engineering credibility through exact alignment, consistent rhythm, and motion that lands on frame. Sloppy spacing or jittery animation would undermine the message that velixo.io delivers precision work.

Voice: confident but not loud. The site doesn't shout "look what I can do" — it simply does it, and the craft speaks.

## Anti-references

- **Generic AI-generated look**: Avoid the templated SaaS landing page aesthetic — identical card grids, gradient text, tiny uppercase eyebrows above every section, numbered section markers as scaffolding, cream/sand backgrounds. If someone could guess "AI made that" without doubt, it has failed.
- **Cheap/amateur portfolios**: No flat colors without depth, no poor typography hierarchy, no missing motion, no default font stacks.
- **Overly aggressive racing cliché**: No neon stripes, flame graphics, excessive carbon-fiber textures, or tacky racing aesthetic. The car is premium; the site should match that tone, not a sticker-bombed tuner shop.

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell** — The site IS the portfolio. Every interaction, animation, and visual choice demonstrates craft. Don't add a section that says "we make great websites" — make the website great.
2. **Practice what you preach** — If velixo.io sells premium web design, this site must be the proof. No shortcut that a client would notice.
3. **Motion as material** — Animation isn't decoration; it's part of the build. Scroll-driven transitions, parallax, and micro-interactions should feel intentional and smooth, never tacked on.
4. **Consistency builds trust** — Color, spacing, typography, and motion should follow a coherent system. The "RS" in the hero, the CTA buttons, and the accent details should all speak the same visual language.
5. **Restraint at the edges** — Premium doesn't mean busy. Negative space, single-accent color discipline, and selective motion create more impact than filling every corner.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA compliance: body text contrast ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1 against backgrounds.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` must be respected for all scroll animations, parallax, and micro-interactions — provide crossfade or instant alternatives.
- Keyboard navigation for all interactive elements (buttons, FAQ accordions, contact form).
- Color choices must not rely on hue alone to convey meaning.
